About Me

Hi, I’m Serena, a Master of Business Analytics student at UBC Sauder with a background in data analytics, user behavior analysis, and quantitative research. I enjoy exploring how people use data and turning complex information into clear, actionable insights.

Using Python, R, SQL, and Tableau, I’ve worked on projects spanning customer engagement, dashboard optimization, and resource allocation. I bring a research-driven mindset and a collaborative approach to solving business problems, connecting data with the people and decisions behind it.

Before UBC, I studied at Whitman College in the US, where I double majored in Economics-Mathematics and Music Production. Music has always been a big part of my life. I play guitar, piano, and a little drums, and I sang in the college chorale.

I also explored music theory and history through coursework and research. Above all, I love making music and have been creating original tracks and songs using digital audio workstations.
